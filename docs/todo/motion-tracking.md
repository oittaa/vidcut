# Motion tracking

## Masks and titles

- Keyframes on the points of a polygon window.
- A connection of a Center that has keyframes.
- A title or a text node that follows a path.
- A clip that follows the path of another clip, for example a picture in a picture.
- A path for a subject with its matte.

## Finds

- Face finds on the GPU.
- Faces under 10 pixels in a wide shot.
- Number plates, animals and screens as subjects.
- Text finds on number plates: not checked.
- The vehicle subject: not checked on a clip with vehicles.
- One person can get two paths.
- The person and vehicle model sees a square, not the shape of the picture.
- The Advanced fields of a find do not stay after the app closes.
- Very small text in a large source.
- Text that comes into the picture: not checked.
- Text finds on the GPU.

## The Tracker

- The pattern flipbooks and the channel bars of the Inspector.
- The handle at the top left of the box of a Point pattern.
- Path Center with Track Center (Append), and the path tools of the viewer.
- A track that follows a turn or a change of perspective.
- Keyframes on X Offset and Y Offset.
- The Tracker Offset button of the viewer, and Select Time with Go.
- The Blend of a Tracker.
- Corner Positioning and Perspective Positioning.
- The Filter Method menu and Flatten Transform.
- Keyframes on the values of the Operation tab.
- The weights of paths of different lengths: not checked against Resolve.
- The outputs Perspective Position, Steady and Unsteady.
- The Options tab: the names of the patterns, enlarge on drag and the search scale.
- The track buttons, Find, Blur, Connect To and Follow Size: not checked in the app.

## The Color page and files

- The Tracker palette of the Color page.
- Follow FX Tracker does not change the picture.
- Motion paths in OpenTimelineIO files.
- The Tracker: not checked against Resolve.
