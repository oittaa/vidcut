# Titles

- The letters of a Text title are up to one pixel out of place.
- Text with Zoom X different from Zoom Y: not checked against Resolve.
- Tracking with a fraction of a pixel at a zoom: not checked against Resolve.
- The pad of a text block when a letter at an end has ink outside its advance.
- Scroll letters from a font in points are smaller than in Resolve.
- A face of a variable font by its own family name, for example "Bahnschrift Light".
- The same fallback font as Resolve for a letter that the font does not have.
- Edit in place of one line of a lower third.
- Tab that types a tab character in a title.
- A spell check.
- The end of a burst of typing: not checked against Resolve.
