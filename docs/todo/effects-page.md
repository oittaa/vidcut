# The Effects page

- Set Color, Versions and Pin in the node header of the Inspector.
- The Settings tab of a tool and of a mask.
- Output Size of a mask.
- The ends, Position and Length in the Border Style of a rectangle.
- The joins of a rectangle with round corners: not checked against Resolve.
- Fit Mask of a picture on an effect mask.
- Keyframes on Low and High of a picture on an effect mask.
- The tile color of each tool: not checked against Resolve.
- = and − to zoom the viewer, and Ctrl+1 for 100 percent.
- Ctrl+F for Find, Ctrl+Shift+V to paste an instance, and Ctrl+G to make a group.
- A render range other than the In and the Out of the clip.
- The Render Start and Render End fields.
- Set the render range by a Ctrl-drag on the ruler, a drag of a yellow line or a drag of a node.
- Auto Render Range and Set to Output Range in the menu of the ruler.
- Play, Ctrl+Left and Ctrl+Right at the ends of the render range.
- Playback Update and the frame increment in the menu of Play Forward.
- Mute and the sound source menu of the Audio button.
- The sound of a clip with a speed change.
