# Effects page

- The Settings tab of tools and masks, and Set Color, Versions and Pin in the node header.
- The rest of the rectangle and picture masks: Border Style, Output Size, Fit Mask, and keyed Low and High.
- A render range: its fields, its drags on the ruler, and Play at its ends.
- The keys of the viewer and the nodes: zoom, Find, paste instance and group.
- Sound on the Effects page: Mute, the source menu, and the sound of a retimed clip.
