# Subject masks

- The Magic Mask palette of the Color page: the click tools, the Click list, Mask Overlay and Quality.
- The track buttons, Stop Tracking and the timeline of the Click list.
- A switch for `color.magic_mask_large` in Preferences > Color.
- The viewer does not make a missing map again.
- After a change before the node and a lost map, an export can show another mask than the viewer.
- Smart Refine.
- Consistency.
- Matte Finesse, with one set of controls for the qualifier and the Magic Mask.
- Paint: the add brush, the subtract brush and the brush size.
- The Alpha Output of the Node Editor.
- Render in Place Magic Mask.
- A Magic Mask node on the Effects page.
- A usable track without a GPU.
- Faster tracks on the GPU.
- The DAM4SAM and SAMURAI memory choices.
- The mask rules (the key, the edge, the subjects, the parts of a person, the tracks): not checked against Resolve.
