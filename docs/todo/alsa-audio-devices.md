# Audio devices on ALSA: a device chosen and unplugged

**When:** next time on a Linux desktop with a USB audio device to unplug.

Choose the USB device in File > Preferences, Player, Sound, then unplug it. vidcut should play on the default and say so (the status bar's notice, `player state`'s `audio_error`), and play on the device again once it is back and chosen.
