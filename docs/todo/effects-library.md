# Effects library

**When:** not decided; before a second kind of effect joins the titles in a tab of its own.

The Titles tab ([spec §2](../cut-page-spec.md#2-layout-pp-636637), top bar; p. 762) becomes one Effects tab that holds everything added from a browser: titles, transitions and effects.

- **Resolve:** the Cut page has separate tabs on the left for the Media Pool, Sync Bin, Keyframe Editor, Transitions, Titles and Effects (p. 636). The Edit page has one Effects Library instead: a list of categories at the left, Toolbox showing all of its Transitions, Titles, Generators and Effects at once, and the chosen category's contents at the right (p. 802).
- **vidcut:** one Effects tab in the Titles tab's place, shaped like the Edit page's Effects Library: its categories (All, Titles, Transitions, Effects; Generators once there are any) and their cards, dragged onto a timeline or double-clicked as title cards are now. Today it would hold the title kinds and Cross Dissolve. A deviation from the Cut page's separate tabs, to record when built: one place to look, and a short tab bar.
- **Open:** which effects come first ([cut-page-gaps](cut-page-gaps.md)), and how a clip effect's card applies: dropped on a clip, or double-clicked onto the selected clips.
