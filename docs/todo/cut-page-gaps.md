# Cut-page behavior that vidcut does not have

What [the spec](../cut-page-spec.md) describes and vidcut does not do.

- Metadata view cards: rows for Date Created, Scene, Shot, Take and Comment, and the Comment field ([§3](../cut-page-spec.md#3-media-pool-ch-34-pp-653675))
- Metadata view: clusters of Timecode and Date Imported, and main descriptions of Timecode, Date Modified and Date Imported ([§3](../cut-page-spec.md#3-media-pool-ch-34-pp-653675))
- Zoom of the waveform in the Source Clip scroll area, once measured ([§4.1](../cut-page-spec.md#41-modes))
