- Display Individual Audio Channels in the clip menu of the timeline.
- Convert to Linked Group for a stereo track.
- Audio track layers, Range mode and sample editing.
- Elastic Wave.
- The Mixer strip of a track: arm, inserts and the Dialogue Leveler.
- Buses, sends and VCAs.
- Presets and Sidechain of the track equalizer and Dynamics.
- Automation of the fader, the pan, the mute and the effects.
- The audio effects of the Effects Library.
- VST and AU plugins.
- Bus meters and Loudness History.
- Recording and ADR.
- The Sound Library.
- Dolby Atmos, Ambisonics and MPEG-H.
- The Pitch and the Music Remixer of a clip in the Inspector.
- The Ducker as Resolve has it: Sound level by default, its fall shapes and fall starts, and gain steps of 16 samples. Also the Source 1 of a new Ducker, and a typed value past a limit set to the limit.
- A clip on a mono track pans by the law of a mono track. A Pan outside −100 to 100 goes to the nearest limit.
