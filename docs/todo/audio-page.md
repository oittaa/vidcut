# Audio page

- The Audio page: the tall track view, the Mixer and the meters on one page.
- Link and unlink of clips, Linked Selection, and the sync offset of linked clips.
- Audio tracks: a lane and a clip for each channel, layers, Range mode, sample editing and Elastic Wave.
- Fade handles on clips, and Batch Fades.
- The Mixer: arm, the solo modes, track pan, inserts, the Dialogue Leveler, buses, sends and VCAs.
- Presets and Sidechain of the track equalizer and Dynamics.
- Automation of the fader, the pan, the mute and the effects.
- The audio effects of the Effects Library, and VST and AU plugins.
- A live loudness meter, bus meters and Loudness History.
- Recording, ADR, the Sound Library and the immersive formats.
