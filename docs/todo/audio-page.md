# Audio page

- The Audio page: the tall track view, the Mixer and the meters on one page.
- A lane and a sound clip for each channel of a multichannel track.
- Audio track layers, Range mode and sample editing.
- Elastic Wave.
- Fade handles on clips, and Batch Fades.
- The Mixer strip of a track: arm, the solo modes, inserts and the Dialogue Leveler.
- Buses, sends and VCAs.
- Presets and Sidechain of the track equalizer and Dynamics.
- Automation of the fader, the pan, the mute and the effects.
- The audio effects of the Effects Library.
- VST and AU plugins.
- Bus meters and Loudness History.
- Recording and ADR.
- The Sound Library.
- Dolby Atmos, Ambisonics and MPEG-H.
