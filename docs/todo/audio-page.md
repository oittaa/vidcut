# The audio page

## Sound on audio tracks

- Rows of the audio tracks on the Cut page.
- The sync offset of a link group that is out of sync.
- Link and unlink clips.
- Linked Selection on and off.
- Slip into place, Move into place, Slip others into place and Move others into place.
- Resync Clip on the Cut page.
- One sound clip for each channel of a stream with more than two channels.
- The audio track that an edit of sound alone goes to: not checked against Resolve.
- New sound over a used audio track: not checked against Resolve.

## Page

- An Audio page with tall rows, the Mixer and the meters together.
- Index, Groups and Metadata panels.
- Track header items: color, number, format, fader value, clip count, arm and meter.
- Waveform zoom and Smart Zoom.
- Lock Track Height To.
- Transient detection, the tempo grid, the scrollers and zoom presets.
- Flags.

## Timeline edits

- A lane for each channel of a track.
- Layered Audio Editing, Show Audio Track Layers and Flatten Audio Track Layers.
- Range mode and Focus mode.
- Cut, copy and paste of a range, also of a part of a clip, on several tracks.
- Trim to Selection, Duplicate Selection, nudge and subframe nudge.
- Paste and Remove Attributes of a track.
- EQ Matcher, Level Matcher and AI Dialogue Matcher.
- Checkerboard to New Tracks.
- Folder tracks and Link Groups.
- Sample editing and the zero crossing line.
- Export Audio Files and External Audio Process.
- Voice Convert and Speech Generator.
- Elastic Wave.

## Fades

- Fade handles at the ends of a clip, with a curve, X-Level and X-Point.
- Fade In to Playhead and Fade Out to Playhead.
- Batch Fades.
- A Cross Fade with a different Fade In and Fade Out level.

## Inspector

- Pitch of a clip.
- Track mode.
- Audio Configuration of each channel: Active, Mute, Level and Source.

## Tracks

- Track color, from 16 colors.
- Duplicate Track, Move Track Up and Down, Delete Empty Tracks and Disable Track.
- Audio track formats: mono, stereo, 5.1, 7.1 and adaptive.
- Change Track Type.

## Mixer

- Arm, Solo Safe and Exclusive Solo.
- Input menu and Path Settings: Trim Level, Polarity and Direct Output.
- Dialogue Leveler on a track.
- Dialogue Separator and Music Remixer.
- Insert slots on tracks and clips, with Effects Order and Effects In.
- Dynamics, equalizer and inserts on the main strip.
- Pan of a track, with the 2D and 3D panners.
- Buses, Bus Format, Bus Outputs and Bus Sends.
- Bus strips with Dynamics, equalizer, inserts and a fader.
- Patch Input/Output.
- Nested timelines.
- VCAs and track groups.
- Bounce Selected Tracks and Bounce Mix to Track.
- AI Audio Assistant.
- Presets Library.

## Track equalizer

- Gain fader.
- Presets.
- Copy and Paste in the "..." menu, also to and from a clip equalizer.
- L, ML, MH and H buttons.
- Some pass filter slopes: not checked against Resolve.

## Track Dynamics

- Presets.
- Sidechain of the Compressor, with On, Listen and Source.
- Moving level on the graph of the window.
- The Compressor at the ratios 1.2 and 1.6 is 0.1 dB higher than Resolve.
- Mix, the Knee, the Expander and most control values: not checked against Resolve.

## Equalizer and Dynamics

- Sample rates other than 48 kHz: not checked against Resolve.

## Automation

- Fader, mute and pan automation lanes of a track.
- Automation of the equalizer, Dynamics, sends, inserts and plugins.
- Automation Follows Edit.
- Automation toolbar: Write, Trim, Touch, On Stop and the enables.
- Preview mode, Active Range and Mix List.
- Audio Panning to Video.

## Effects

- Common FX controls: presets, A/B, Reset and Bypass.
- Noise Reduction on a track.
- Limiter, Gain, De-Esser, De-Hummer, Multiband Compressor, Vocal Channel and Dialogue Processor.
- Chorus, Delay, Distortion, Echo, Flanger, Modulation, Pitch, Reverb, Soft Clipper, Stereo Fixer and Stereo Width.
- Chain FX and the Fairlight EQ insert.
- Meter, Phase Meter, Frequency Analyzer and Surround Analyzer FX.
- VST and AU plugins, their Sidechain, and Cache Audio Effects.

## Meters

- A live loudness meter: M, Short, Short Max, Range, Integrated and true peak.
- Target Loudness Level and loudness scale of the project.
- Bus meters, and a Control Room meter with true peak.
- Loudness History.
- Meter Type and pre fader metering.
- Output level and DIM.

## Other

- Recording, input monitoring and VSTi.
- ADR.
- Sound Library and Foley Sampler.
- Test tones and timecode chase.
- Loop Jog.
- AAF import.
- Control surfaces.
- Immersive formats: Dolby Atmos, Ambisonics and MPEG-H.
