# Sync Bin

- Open a multicam clip as a timeline on the Edit page.
- Switch the angle of a multicam clip with a cut.
- An angle switch that replaces the picture, keeps the chosen sound and is one edit.
- A clip of a synced group on the timeline does not follow Save Sync.
- Sync by Marker in the Sync Clips window.
- The Sync Bin and Multi Source grids in the Cinema Viewer with P.
- Find in Multi Source in the menu of the timeline.
- Live Overwrite by a drag in a cell of Multi Source.
- The red and white outlines of the pool's files in Multi Source.
- The camera name from the tags of a file.
- Sync by sound: not tested with files from real cameras.
- Shift+Comma, far timecode tags and files with no picture in the Sync Clips window: not checked against Resolve.
- The number keys, Escape, the sound, the view of one camera and mixed files in the Sync Bin: not checked against Resolve.
- Source Overwrite with several pool items, or on a new track: not checked against Resolve.
